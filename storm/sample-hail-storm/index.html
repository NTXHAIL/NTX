What to check on your vehicle

Walk around the vehicle in daylight and look across each panel, not straight down at it. Hail often shows up as a field of shallow dents that are easy to miss in a garage.

  • Hood — look from the front edge toward the windshield.
  • Roof — include the area above the doors, not only the center.
  • Trunk — or the tailgate on a truck.
  • Both sides — doors, fenders, and quarter panels.
  • Glass — windshield, back glass, and side windows. Chips and cracks are separate from dents in the metal.

How paintless dent repair works

Paintless dent repair (PDR) pushes hail dents out from behind the panel. When the paint is intact, the factory finish stays on the vehicle: no body filler and no respray on those panels. If a panel is creased or the paint is broken, we say so and explain the honest next step.

NTX Hail is a national auto hail catastrophe company. Technicians deploy to the storm, whether that is one neighborhood or a multi-state event. Send a few phone photos and a technician will reply with an estimate.

Help with your insurance claim

We photograph the panels, explain the estimate, and work with your insurance carrier from the inspection through any supplement. Your policy decides what is covered. Your deductible remains your responsibility. NTX Hail does not waive deductibles or offer rebates. Waiving or rebating a deductible is illegal in Texas, and we do not do it in any state where we work.
